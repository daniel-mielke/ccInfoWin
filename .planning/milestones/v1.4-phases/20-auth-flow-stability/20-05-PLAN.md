Required wrapped form (after this plan):
```xml
<Border Width="30"
        Height="30"
        CornerRadius="6"
        Background="#0F172A"
        HorizontalAlignment="Right"
        VerticalAlignment="Top"
        Margin="8">
    <Button l:Uids.Uid="LoginReloadButton"
            Click="OnReloadLoginClicked"
            Background="Transparent"
            BorderThickness="0"
            Padding="0"
            CornerRadius="6"
            HorizontalAlignment="Stretch"

        <FontIcon Glyph="&#xE72C;"
                  FontSize="16"
                  Foreground="{ThemeResource SecondaryTextBrush}" />
    </Button>
</Border>
```

Notes on the wrapped form:
- Outer <Border> owns the contrast surface (Width=30, Height=30, Background=#0F172A, CornerRadius=6).
- Outer <Border> takes over the alignment+margin from the previous Button (HorizontalAlignment=Right, VerticalAlignment=Top, Margin=8) — these moved up one level.
- Inner Button keeps its identity (l:Uids.Uid, Click handler, Background=Transparent, BorderThickness=0, CornerRadius=6, FontIcon child unchanged).
- Inner Button Padding becomes 0 because the Border now provides the 30x30 hit-target; with original Padding=8 + FontSize=16 the bounding box would already be ~32x32, exceeding the 30x30 wrapper. Padding=0 inside a 30x30 Border with a 16 px FontIcon yields a centered glyph with ~7 px breathing room on each side — visually balanced and within WinUI 3's 32x32 minimum hit target (the Border itself receives the click via its hosted Button).
- Inner Button alignment becomes Stretch/Stretch so it fills the Border exactly (no offset, no clipping).
- Background=#0F172A is a literal Slate-900 hex (NOT a theme brush) per planning_context note: contrast must be guaranteed against the cream-white WebView2 content regardless of OS theme. ApplicationPageBackgroundThemeBrush would flip to white in light theme and defeat the fix.
